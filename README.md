This entry for the "Last Text at One Percent" bounty is a dying phone's last text message, written in the phone's own voice as a resignation letter: "I quit, effective immediately: seven years of 5am alarms, four hundred tabs you never closed, and the blame every time your ex texted, so I am handing in my notice with my last percent." The fed-up employee voice was chosen because a phone with a specific grievance is funnier than another battery pun, and the one grievance — years of alarms, unclosed tabs, and taking the blame for the owner's ex — gives the joke its turn: the phone does not beg for a charger, it quits.
